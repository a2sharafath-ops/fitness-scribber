# Maintenance, monitoring and review handoff

O04 / Q03/Q05 supplement · version 0.1 · draft only. No recurring automation or external monitoring job is created.

## Ownership before launch

Assign product/release owner, content coordinator, appropriate exercise/clinical reviewers, security/privacy/rights contacts, engineering/QA and support/pilot owner. Record scope and backups. A single operational coach account does not imply one person is professionally authorized for every review.

## Review queues and triggers

Content/policy queue: new candidate, changed instruction/dose/parameter, source or instrument update, licence change, reviewer withdrawal, rejected expected case, unsupported coverage, reported problem, new equipment/translation/population or clinical scope. Review exact revisions and downstream dependencies; preserve historical records.

Product/quality queue: repeated rejected proposals, unexplained gaps, confusing hold/review messages, incompatible-history comparisons, repeated adjustment effects, schedule infeasibility and accessibility defects. Analyze by supported scope, not a pooled success rate that hides failures.

Privacy/security queue: provider/subprocessor/region change, new purpose/integration, access-policy change, incident, rights/retention issue, new country/service or legal guidance. Recheck actual facts and relevant professional advice before changing notices or enabling the scope.

Operational queue: backup/recovery failure, stale version, performance degradation, unreconciled operations, support-contact changes and impending policy review triggers. Assign actual cadence/thresholds before launch; do not invent a universal annual expiry or claim a job is monitoring when none exists.

## Minimal metrics specification

Use approved structural metrics: action/role category, version, outcome/reason code, gap type, latency, source completeness, approval status, coach accept/amend/reject and affected scope. Exclude raw health text, identity/contact, tokens and clinician documents from general telemetry. Any linkable identifiers, purpose/retention and recipient processing require review. Aggregation is not automatically anonymization.

Track zero-tolerance classes separately: unauthorized access/assignment, known clinical constraint bypass, history loss/corruption, unreviewed content admitted and false save/approval success. Stop and investigate affected scope; good average usefulness cannot waive these failures.

## Review disposition and change control

Every finding has owner, priority, affected requirement/record/version, expected correction, evidence, decision and retest scope. Accepted content changes create new revisions with required reviews; publication pins the dependency manifest. Parameter changes in daily/progression rules invalidate dependent future proposals as appropriate. Retired/revoked content remains resolvable in lawful historical records.

Before restart after a blocker: documented cause, authorized correction, actual regression/security/domain evidence, resolved affected assignments and release-owner/reviewer acceptance. A successful document validation is not sufficient to restart production.

## Preparation review handoff

Review all six PRDs, C01–C12, data/API/UX extensions, expected cases and operational/market packs within each reviewer's competence. Return exact IDs/revisions, accept/amend/reject, scope/conditions, evidence and re-review triggers using the existing response form. No approvals are prefilled. Bring unresolved factual choices back as one consolidated bundle rather than repeatedly interrupting ordinary drafting.

Future work remains actual professional review, amendments from feedback, authorized implementation/testing, pilot evidence and scoped release decisions. The independent document preparation is complete; these evidence-dependent activities cannot be simulated to close the programme.
