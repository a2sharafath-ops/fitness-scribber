# Coach, client and support guide — draft

O03 / U02 · version 0.1. Proposed future product behavior, not confirmation that these controls exist in the current app. Adapt final help to the verified build before publication.

## Coach workflow

Review current client context and required issues first. Confirm actual goal order, session time/kit/support and task prerequisites. Use the pool to inspect eligible options and reasons; a draft candidate or score does not grant clinical clearance. Select a session purpose, review side/dose/source/time and required gaps, then approve the exact current revision. Manual/copy/template/voice content follows the same checks.

For daily adjustments, compare the original session with the proposed changes and evidence quality. Resolve competing proposals before approval; multiple wellness signals must not create hidden compounded reductions. For progression, inspect comparable actual history and why other sessions were excluded. A missed session does not by itself mean regression is needed. Weekly drafts may remain incomplete if constraints cannot be satisfied.

Where a clinical instruction is needed, record genuine scoped evidence and the appropriate reviewer. Do not clear a restriction simply because a form is old, a client completed a workout or an uploaded note exists. The review process is separate from coach approval of a workout.

## Client workflow

Use your own account. Provide relevant information you know and correct mistakes; unanswered fields are not assumed normal. Before a session, complete the required health-change check. Optional wellness is separate and may be skipped. Report changed equipment, available time or support and request an adjustment; your coach reviews material plan changes.

Follow only the applicable coach-approved instructions. You may stop, report a concern, ask questions or request a modification. The app is not an emergency service or continuously monitored; use appropriate local help for urgent concerns instead of waiting for an app reply. Exact health/urgent-help wording must be professionally reviewed before publication.

Saved, submitted and reviewed are different states. A locally stopped session does not prove the report reached the coach. If verification is unavailable, the new design does not authorize assignment/start/resume. Pending actuals must be labelled clearly; do not repeatedly submit a possibly committed operation as a new request.

## Support triage

| Report | First check / non-destructive response | Escalation |
|---|---|---|
| No exercise options | Required versus optional gap, equipment/prerequisite/source state, accepted scope | Coach/content owner; do not force an unreviewed fallback |
| Plan needs review | Context/policy/prescription version and new reports | Coach or appropriate professional; support cannot clear clinical holds |
| Save not confirmed | Known failure versus unknown committed outcome; original operation status | Engineering; preserve edits and avoid duplicate operations |
| Wrong side/load/history | Preserve evidence and stop affected future use | Coach/domain + engineering; do not overwrite completed actuals |
| Unexpected client data visible | Restrict affected access and preserve minimal evidence | Security/privacy incident owner; no wider data sharing |
| Consent/rights request | Verify identity proportionately, identify purpose and applicable process | Privacy owner; do not require exercise participation to access rights |
| Content error | Exact variant/policy revision and affected scope | Content/clinical/rights owner as relevant; controlled revocation/review |
| Offline/quota problem | Explain unsaved/local status; do not bypass server checks | Engineering/support; no claim of safe authoritative offline execution |

## Scope and communications

Support access is least-privilege and attributable; a support role is not unrestricted health-history access. Use minimal structured issue descriptions and approved safe messages. Do not paste credentials or client medical notes into public bug reports. Client/reviewer/regulator communications require assigned responsibility and actual authority; no messages are sent by this preparation work.

Help publication requires verified UI labels/routes, accessible formats, service response expectations, monitored contacts and accepted regional wording. Contacts, service hours and legal deadlines remain pending facts rather than fabricated promises.
