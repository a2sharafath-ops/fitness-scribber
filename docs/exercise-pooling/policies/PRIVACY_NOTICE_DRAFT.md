## Who is responsible

[OPERATOR] uses the coach workspace and client app to deliver the coaching services agreed with you. This notice explains information you provide, your coach records during coaching, or an authorized professional/provider supplies for a specified purpose.

Your account is personal to you. Do not share the coach's credentials or another client's account. A clinician independently providing treatment may have their own privacy notice and record responsibilities; this notice does not replace those.

## Information and purposes

We collect information relevant to your agreed service, not every category for every client:

| Information | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Identity, age eligibility, contact and account details | Identify the correct client, provide access, communicate and manage requests. |
| Relevant screening answers, symptoms, medicines, limitations and professional instructions | Review coaching suitability, record restrictions and support appropriately scoped programming. |
| Fitness/movement assessments and relevant measurements | Establish a baseline, inform coach-reviewed options and track agreed goals. |
| Goals, experience, equipment, setting, preferences and accessibility needs | Make training practical and appropriate to the agreed service. |
| Workouts, completion, effort and feedback | Deliver/review coaching and proposed adjustments. |
| Lifestyle/wellness entries you choose to provide | Discuss recovery, scheduling and training context; these are not medical diagnoses. |
| Communications, consent and approval history | Respond to you and document applicable instructions, choices and approvals. |
| Necessary technical/security records | Operate accounts, investigate faults/unauthorized access and meet applicable obligations without unnecessary health details in logs. |

Optional sources such as wearables, photographs, videos or clinician reports will be described when offered. We explain what is necessary for a requested feature, what is optional and the effect of declining. If essential screening information is unavailable, the coach may need to pause or limit the affected service rather than guess.

## How information informs workouts

Software may organize information and suggest exercise options or drafts. Your coach reviews and approves assignment. Suggestions do not independently diagnose conditions, grant clearance or replace treating-professional instructions. You can ask your coach to explain or reconsider a recommendation and correct inaccurate source information.
