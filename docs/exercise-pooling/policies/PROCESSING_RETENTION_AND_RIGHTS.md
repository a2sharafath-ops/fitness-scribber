# Processing, retention, rights and incident specification

Q05 / O03 / U02 · v0.2 · operational proposal, not present compliance or an executed deletion policy.

Legal basis and any additional health-data condition must be selected for each purpose and jurisdiction by the appropriate reviewer. Contract alone is not a blanket basis for health data; consent is not automatically the correct or sufficient basis. The proposed no sale/health-targeted advertising/external model-training policy still requires adoption and verification against actual providers before publication.

## Provider/data-flow verification

Local source establishes React/Vite, Supabase-related code and a local-storage path. It does not establish live database regions, processor contracts, backups, operational support access or all enabled integrations. Verify database/auth, Vercel or other hosting, email/invite delivery, crash/analytics, external video/voice/AI, exports and any wearable integrations independently. Do not inspect hosted accounts or client records under this preparation approval.

For each actual processor: legal entity, service/purpose, data categories, controller/processor role, region and subprocessors, contract/DPA, access controls, deletion/backups, retention, transfer mechanism and incident support contact. A brand name is not a complete provider schedule. External embedded media may cause browser requests; do not equate a link with authorization to send health records.

## Retention and deletion design

The approved preparation default is a purpose/trigger-based schedule with numeric periods left for the known operator, service and jurisdiction; no invented statutory number. Before any retention job is built, complete for each category: start trigger, active period, post-service period, exception/legal hold authority, backup expiry, deletion/anonymization method, accountable owner and evidence. Unknown periods block that job's enablement and policy publication, not all drafting.

Deletion must cover primary stores, derived pool/context caches, approved local/browser copies, exports under operator control and processor copies within verified capabilities. Do not promise to erase recipients' lawful independent records or inaccessible copies. Backup handling requires a documented expiry and suppression/reapplication procedure so deleted records are not silently restored to live service. Genuine anonymization requires review; pseudonymous identifiers alone are not anonymous data.

Preserve necessary audit/history according to lawful retention with minimized fields and restricted access, rather than deleting an exercise identity to satisfy an unrelated optional-data withdrawal. Do not run any deletion, anonymization, migration or backup operation from this document.

## Rights and consent workflow

Receive request through the verified app route or monitored contact; acknowledge, proportionately verify identity/representative authority, classify scope/jurisdiction and applicable deadline, locate primary/derived/processor records, assess lawful exceptions, perform authorized action, record result and respond with explanation/escalation route. Assign a responsible person and legal escalation route before launch. Avoid collecting new sensitive identity documents when a lower-risk account verification suffices.

Consent evidence is purpose-specific and versioned: actual client/authorized representative action, collection method, presented notice/form, timestamp and receipt. The coach may record a genuinely obtained external consent with evidence, but cannot impersonate the client by ticking their choices. Any material new purpose needs its own applicable authority; older participation acknowledgement is not blanket permission.

Withdrawal of optional sharing/media stops that purpose prospectively without automatically stopping unrelated coaching. Withdrawal/loss of required processing authority prevents affected new processing/planning until reviewed; it does not retrospectively invalidate lawful prior use or automatically erase legally required retained records. Continue privacy/support access and the ability to stop/report. Inform the client of genuine service consequences and alternatives without forcing unrelated consent.

## Incident response draft

Named owner/backup contacts remain pending. Proposed procedure: detect/report and record minimal facts; contain affected access/feature without deleting evidence; preserve restricted incident evidence; identify data, people, systems/regions and authorized recipients affected; consult security/privacy/legal and relevant clinical lead; determine applicable notifications and deadlines; send only under approved authority; recover with access/restore checks; document lessons and required re-review. Do not use a universal 72-hour promise across all markets or assume an app outside HIPAA has no health-breach duties.

Test later with synthetic cases: cross-client response, leaked medical note in error log, lost device/browser copy, wrong recipient export, lost save response, revoked token, restore of deleted optional data and unacknowledged client concern. Classify legal incident duties separately from ordinary app errors and from clinical reports. This app is not a live emergency-monitoring service.

## Publication acceptance

No placeholder operator/contact, unknown actual provider/region, unresolved purpose/basis, unapproved retention schedule or inaccessible rights route may appear as a completed client notice. Before publication: match forms to UI/API/storage; verify consent/withdrawal tests; approve country addenda and service claims; assign operational contacts; approve actual release versions. Keep the current privacy/participation/sharing drafts labelled internal until then.
