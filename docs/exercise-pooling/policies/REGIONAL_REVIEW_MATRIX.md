# International launch — regional review matrix

Package supplement 0.2, 2026-09-05. Intended markets are user-confirmed; exact countries/states, establishment, services and activation dates are not. This is initial research/review work, not approved market addenda or legal certification.

Use a shared truthful core notice plus reviewed regional disclosures, consent, rights and transfer arrangements. Listing a market in a policy does not authorize launch. Product approval, professional authority and privacy acceptance are separate.

## Primary-source anchors checked

- India: the Rules phase commencement; check the Act timeline and corrigendum, not just enactment. [Rules](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf), [official index](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digit), [SPDI clarification](https://www.meity.gov.in/writereaddata/files/PressNote_25811.pdf).
- EU reach: establishment and targeting/monitoring can matter for a non-EU business. [European Commission](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/reform/rules-business-and-organisations/application-regulation/who-does-data-protection-law-apply_en?data1=hiip).
- UK health information: an ordinary lawful basis and an additional special-category condition must be distinguished; a “rehabilitation” label does not itself establish a healthcare basis. [ICO](https://ico.org.uk/for-organisations/uk-gdpr-guidance-and-resources/lawful-basis/special-category-data/what-are-the-rules-on-special-category-data/?q=dpa).
- US HIPAA depends on actual entity relationships, not merely storing health information. [HHS health-app guidance](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/special-topics/health-apps/index.html), [entity definitions](https://www.hhs.gov/hipaa/for-professionals/covered-entities/index.html?hl=en).
- Other US rules: FTC guidance discusses records capable of drawing information from multiple sources, relevant to assessing wearable-capable apps. [FTC guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-ftcs-health-breach-notification-rule-0). Washington is one example of a separate state consumer-health-data regime. [Washington Attorney General](https://www.atg.wa.gov/protecting-washingtonians-personal-health-data-and-privacy).
- UAE official guidance identifies health-sector ICT legislation; review applicability before assuming standard overseas hosting is sufficient. [UAE government](https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/whole-of-government-approach/digital-health/ict-in-the-health-sector).
- Saudi implementing rules contain health-data-specific provisions. [SDAIA implementing rules](https://dgp.sdaia.gov.sa/wps/portal/pdp/knowledgecenter/details/PDPL2), [official English PDPL](https://sdaia.gov.sa/en/SDAIA/about/Documents/Personal%20Data%20English%20V2-23April2023-%20Reviewed-.pdf).

These sources support differentiated review; they do not establish current app compliance. All six GCC countries now have primary-source research dossiers in the [regional review pack](./REGIONAL_REVIEW_PACK.md). Exact US states and unspecified non-EU European countries still require selection-specific research before activation. No worldwide, HIPAA, GDPR or DPDP compliance claim is made.

## Proposed market activation evidence

Record operator/establishment; actual client location/services; professional authority; data categories/purposes/grounds; providers/regions/transfers; notices/language/accessibility/consent; rights/complaints/retention/incident procedures; actual reviewer acceptance; security tests; scoped pilot/rollback permission; and the final market enablement decision. A general international-launch approval cannot replace these specific records.
