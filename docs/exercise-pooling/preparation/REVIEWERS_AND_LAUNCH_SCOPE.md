# Reviewer and launch-scope recommendations

Version: 0.1 draft, 2026-09-05. Supports P01, D04, C04, C12 and Q05. This is a recommendation, not an appointment, medical approval or legal opinion.

## Recommended approval responsibilities

| Decision/content | Recommended accountable approver | What they actually review |
|---|---|---|
| Product goals, workflow and acceptance | Project owner / operating coach | Intended users, features, commercial scope, usability and final product acceptance. |
| General and advanced exercise catalogue | Appropriately qualified exercise professional with relevant advanced strength-and-conditioning experience | Technique, prerequisites, progression families, loading/dose templates and supported population. Owner may contribute within verified competence; obtain a second content review for engine rules. |
| Musculoskeletal rehabilitation and return-to-training content | Physiotherapist appropriately qualified and authorized to practise in the service jurisdiction | Clinical pathway boundaries, permitted coach actions, condition-specific limits, post-operative/return-to-training constraints and referral criteria within that professional's scope. |
| Medical screening and systemic/complex conditions | Physician with appropriate sports/exercise/rehabilitation or condition-specific competence; clinical exercise physiologist can contribute within their role | Medical screening/referral logic, medically supervised exercise requirements and case-specific clearance where indicated. A physiotherapy review is not blanket approval of every cardiac/metabolic/neurological case. |
| Adaptive exercise and accessibility | Professional experienced with the relevant functional needs, with input from affected clients and the relevant clinician when necessary | Transfers, assistance, environment, communication, balance/support and individual accommodations. Do not use disability labels as automatic contraindications. |
| Privacy, consent, rights and service claims | Adviser familiar with the operating jurisdiction and health-data services | Operator identity, lawful basis, current law, commencement dates, notices, retention, processors, permissions, clinical-service boundary and contract wording. |
| Technical quality/security | Assistant prepares evidence; competent engineering/security reviewer where needed | Data preservation, permissions, backend bypasses, correctness, reliability and release risks. This does not substitute for legal or clinical review. |

ACSM distinguishes personal training, exercise physiology and clinical exercise physiology roles. Its credential is an example of relevant competence, not a universal licence to practise in every country. [ACSM role descriptions](https://acsm.org/certification/get-certified/)

India's NCAHP Act includes physiotherapy within its professional framework. If India is selected, verify the individual's qualifications, current registration/authorization where applicable, scope and local requirements rather than assuming a certificate title is sufficient. [India Code: NCAHP Act](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/16824?col=123456789%2F1362&view_type=search)

For a single-coach business these can be part-time/advisory reviews, not new full-time hires. Nobody is hired or contacted by this recommendation. Do not seek one blanket approval covering all special populations; commission scoped review of the pathways actually offered. Confirm credentials, relevant experience, conflicts, scope of engagement and an auditable review record.

## Approval has three separate levels

1. **Catalogue/policy approval:** a professional reviews a rule or exercise template for a defined population and setting.
2. **Client-specific clinical instructions:** where indicated, the treating/appropriate clinician establishes the individual's permitted activity, limits, supervision and review triggers. An approved generic catalogue is not individual clearance.
3. **Coach assignment approval:** the operating coach reviews the actual generated workout, confirms its suitability within documented constraints and assigns it. This remains required for all clients and training levels.

For R1, clinical reviewers need not have new app accounts: a proposed controlled process can attach their scoped signed/versioned review evidence, recorded by the coach with source and date. Uploading a letter must not automatically mark all restrictions resolved. Document exactly which constraints it addresses and who reviewed that interpretation. This workflow is still awaiting product/domain approval.

## Recommended launch geography

The user has now specified intended markets: India, GCC, USA, UK and Europe. Retain all of these as design targets. The recommendation is still to activate markets in stages, starting where the coaching business is established and appropriately able to deliver the service. If that country is India, propose an India-first pilot for approval, not an India-only product or a silently narrowed scope. The business's legal establishment has not been confirmed; a list of target markets or a development-machine timezone is not proof of establishment.

This is an operational recommendation, not a claim that India has lighter rules or that a privacy notice creates permission to practise. It concentrates the initial consent, service, professional-review and incident procedures in one setting. If the business is elsewhere, select that home market first instead of forcing an India-based policy.

Add another country only when there is actual client demand and an approved market-specific review of service/professional scope, consumer terms, privacy, processors/transfers and incident response. Citizenship alone is not a sufficient jurisdiction test. For example, GDPR can apply to non-EU operators targeting people in the EU; being a one-person business does not by itself remove that issue. [European Commission: territorial application](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/reform/rules-business-and-organisations/application-regulation/who-does-data-protection-law-apply_en?data1=hiip)

Do not claim that a website label saying “India only” automatically avoids other laws. Review where the business and clients are located and what services/monitoring are actually offered. An existing client's travel needs a context check, not an assumption that the app now supports every destination's clinical practice.

## India-specific preparation caveats, if selected

- The DPDP Act provides the statutory framework; enactment alone is not evidence that every provision is currently operative. The 2025 Rules explicitly phase commencement. The publication index also lists an enforcement timeline and a December 2025 corrigendum. Review the applicable instruments and actual launch date before giving legal assurances. [DPDP Act](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf), [Rules, commencement clause](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf), [official publication index](https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa?pageTitle=Digit).
- Include review of the current IT/SPDI framework and its transition/applicability rather than treating DPDP as the only consideration. The government clarification addresses electronic consent and direct service-provider obligations. [Official SPDI clarification](https://www.meity.gov.in/writereaddata/files/PressNote_25811.pdf).
- Do not promise immediate universal erasure or pick retention periods by intuition. Review applicable legal/professional retention, processing/security logs, backups and the dates on which requirements apply. No numeric retention schedule is approved yet.
- Disability does not, by itself, transfer decision-making to a guardian. Verify any legally authorized representative's authority and provide accessible communication; the precise legal process needs jurisdiction-specific review.

## Facts required before policy publication or launch

Legal operator name and address/country; privacy/grievance contact; professional responsibility/competence; services actually offered; client authentication and app packaging; languages/accessibility; current enabled features/processors; hosting/storage regions and contracts; support/admin access; retention and deletion process; applicable consent capture; clinician-sharing process; exact enabled countries/states and policy reviewer. A client app is confirmed. Until the remaining facts are resolved, policy files remain internal drafts with placeholders.
