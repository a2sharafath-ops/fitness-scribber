## Dose decisions

Numeric examples below are fictional time-accounting inputs, not general prescriptions. Client-specific values must be selected and reviewed; the system must not fill unresolved loads/ranges automatically.

## Session templates

- SESSION-01 — Full-body resistance plus preparation: Choose one eligible option for each coach-required pattern; optional patterns are coach-marked. Do not silently omit a required pull in an equipment-free setting.
- SESSION-02 — Travel constrained full-body: Inventory precedes template. No safe anchor/no pulling option returns a gap or coach-changed session purpose, never an improvised anchor.
- SESSION-03 — Strength-priority session: Coach names principal variant and compatible accessories. Warm-up sets and all rest require explicit dose/time, not a generic percentage.
- SESSION-04 — Upper/lower split slot: Coach selects upper or lower slot and required patterns before composition; do not infer next day or weekly progression.
- SESSION-05 — Conditioning-focused session: Walking may substitute only if coach permits locomotion and relevant access/dose checks pass; no automatic cross-modality intensity conversion.
- SESSION-06 — Task/mobility practice: Choose confirmed side-specific task needs; SMR is separately opted in after review, not mandatory.
- SESSION-07 — Seated-access session: Seated alternatives may be considered only when their own trunk control, transfer, anchoring and arm demands are met; this is not a diagnosis-specific protocol.
- SESSION-08 — Clinician handoff session: Blocked clinical template: requires accepted pathway, qualified reviewer, client-specific instructions and limits. No general catalogue exercise is automatically clinical-approved.
