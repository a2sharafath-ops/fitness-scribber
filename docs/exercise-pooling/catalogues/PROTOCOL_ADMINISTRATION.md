# Protocol administration and competence review

C02 supplement · v0.2. The 15 structured protocol records define data meaning/comparability; this document supplies administration and review boundaries. These are draft collection specifications, not licensed instrument text or an instruction to perform testing on a client.

## Common record requirements

Record protocol and revision, purpose, administrator/source and relevant competence, client/session link, actual assessment time and recorded time, environment/equipment, side, unit/scale, observed state, confirmation/quality, limitations and consent/purpose evidence where applicable. Retain original unit/wording in restricted evidence and derive only reviewed structured projections. Do not capture unnecessary identifiers in test outputs.

Instructions must be accessible and comprehensible. Explain the task, voluntary participation, alternatives and stop/report route. Use the accepted task-specific setup and permitted limits; missing competence, unsafe setup or unresolved clinical concern prevents the affected assessment, not a fabricated zero result. Record not assessed/declined/invalid separately from assessed absence. Clinical evaluation must be performed by someone authorized for it; coaching review is not a diagnosis.

## Acceptance evidence

Before a protocol is adopted: exact procedure/version, lawful use/translation, intended population and exclusions, administrator competence, accessible instructions, scale anchors/units, stopping/referral policy, recording/missingness rules, comparable reassessment criteria and approved expected cases. A registry record with missing adopted procedure is review-ready but cannot produce a clinical or normative interpretation. Formal procedure adoption is still pending; the protocol/source inventory itself is complete for S2 drafting.
